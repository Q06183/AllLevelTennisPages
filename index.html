For coaches

Plan lessons with student progress in context.

Coach mode turns AllLevelTennis into a lightweight teaching workspace for student profiles, skill assessments, schedules, lesson plans, and 10-lesson development blueprints.

  • Manage student profiles with levels, skill progress, and pain-point assessments.
  • Build single-lesson plans from focus skills and recommended corrective drills.
  • View daily lessons on a timeline schedule and adjust time blocks with drag gestures.
  • Create 10-lesson blueprints and export them as shareable images for students.

Private by design

Offline-first, no account required.

Local Storage

The app is designed without a backend server. Training records, notes, student profiles, schedules, and lesson plans are stored locally on your device.

Data Migration

Export a JSON backup and restore it on another device when you need to move your tennis history or coaching workspace.
